Technocrat's Iron, Europa

The Guardian fights through the Salvation Fallen.

The Guardian locates and inspects the cargo.

The Spider slips the Guardian a mysterious key.

*The Drifter:* Mm, that's awfully esoteric for a buncha rubes like these. They gotta be taking orders from someone. Who the hell's calling the shots around here.

The Guardian defeats Keelhal with the help of Spider's crew. They examine Eramis's Stasis prison.
