## Drifter Dialogue (Chronological by Source)

### Weblore: Season of the Drifter (2019) — Drifter & Joxer

"Think of this as a job interview," Drifter replied. "I got a whole new operation about to come online, and Gambit was training for it. You're one of the best candidates I got."

"Why does everyone think I got a mad-on for that one?"

"Hand to my heart, it wasn't just us. Whole buncha folks running around with Malfeasance hand cannons these days. My grandma, too."

"Listen, big guy. Not my fault you keep missing the shot."

"Hey! My bad, brother! Your Motes are always good here, whether you're packing Malfeasance or not. What I need done, only a specialist—like you—can do."

"I seen you out there. You get how the Taken feel. How the Darkness… flows. You revel in it."

"You're a born invader, my friend. The best."

"And I'll need someone like you to test out my new project before I unveil it to the unwashed masses: Gambit Prime."

"You wanna get paid or not?" / "What do I kill?"

"Cheer up, brother. That didn't go too bad."

"In the dirt. Never comin' back. Their Ghosts got sloppy. You give Taken the chance and they'll snuff out your Light. The fact that you're alive means your Ghost knows what he's doin'."

"Where do you think you are? The Crucible? Gambit Prime is for keeps, test or no."

"Leave the name-calling to me, hotshot. Let's wrap up this debrief and get you paid."

"You got yourself out. You didn't need help."

"You could've stopped those Taken. You could've saved them all."

"I paid you to try on that armor. How's it treating you?"

"Brother, if you manage to summon a giant blocker? That thing's gotta eat."

"You found one that matched your strength. Lesson learned. Make sure your Ghost stays on his toes."

"Yeah, in a town full of immortals. Who'da thought?"

"Their cut goes to you. And a little extra to keep, you know, the details outta sight from the Vanguard."

"You can keep the armor."

"How much data did you pick up?"

### Destinypedia — The Drifter (Biography Quotes)

"Haven't seen shooting like that since an Iron Lady named Efrideet ruined my bar. The Dark Age was wild times."

"I think I mentioned we're all raving psychos at this point. Well, we did what all measured raving psychos would do. We thought we each had been betrayed by the others. We drew on each other. To this day, I'm not sure how many of those guys drew intending to kill. But I'll tell you this. I was the only one who walked out."

"He's not like them, but not far off—hard to trust, hard to know. He's seen more than most can imagine and done things most wouldn't dare. But he's always kept his distance—stayed far from the Light." (Shin Malphur about the Drifter)
